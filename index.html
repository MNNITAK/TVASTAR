Leadership

Young leadership, ambition and a forward-looking approach to infrastructure development.

Director | AP Tvastar Buildcon Pvt. Ltd.

Asit Srivastava

Director

At 18, Asit Srivastava represents a new generation of young entrepreneurs entering India's infrastructure and construction industry.

He completed his schooling at City Montessori School, Lucknow, graduating in 2025. He is currently pursuing his undergraduate degree at FLAME University, Pune, majoring in Finance, with minors in Human Resource Management, Legal Studies, and International Relations and Foreign Policy.

His academic foundation across finance, management, law and international affairs provides a multidisciplinary perspective on business strategy and infrastructure development.

His interests include infrastructure financing, project management, business development and the evolving landscape of India's construction sector.

Taking on directorial responsibilities at a young age, Asit reflects the company's emphasis on young leadership, ambition and a forward-looking approach to infrastructure development.

Managing Director | AP Tvastar Buildcon Pvt. Ltd.

Prakhar Srivastava

Managing Director

At 21, Prakhar Srivastava represents a new generation of infrastructure entrepreneurs, combining technical education with practical industry exposure and business leadership.

He completed his schooling at City Montessori School, Lucknow, followed by his education at Birla Public School. He subsequently pursued a B.Tech in Civil Engineering from Bangalore, developing a technical foundation in civil engineering and construction.

Having gained practical industry knowledge and exposure to infrastructure and construction activities from an early age, Prakhar brings technical understanding, operational insight and entrepreneurial vision to AP Tvastar Buildcon Pvt. Ltd.

As Managing Director, he is focused on contributing to the company's growth, strengthening its execution capabilities and advancing its presence across India's infrastructure and construction sectors.

His approach combines engineering knowledge with practical business understanding and a commitment to quality-driven and sustainable infrastructure development.
